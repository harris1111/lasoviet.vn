# AI and evidence

## Current scope

AI does not replace chart facts. P0 evidence is built from life palace, body palace, and transformations only when ruleSetId is ziwei.default; every item carries limitations, risk tags, and allowed actions.

## How to inspect it

Interpretation must stay conditional. It can invite reflection or exploration, but it cannot infer health, legal, financial, or relationship outcomes. Readers can inspect an evidence key instead of trusting an unnamed claim.

## Limitations

This material describes product behavior, data, and public scope only. It is not a certain forecast, diagnosis, or substitute for professional advice.
