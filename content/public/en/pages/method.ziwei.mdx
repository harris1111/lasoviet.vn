# The Tu Vi method

## Current scope

The adapter is pinned to iztro 2.6.0 with ruleSetId ziwei.default. It maps raw palace, branch, star, brightness, and mutagen fields into canonical identifiers used by the shared contract.

## How to inspect it

Fixtures cover calendar conversion, leap months, early and late Zi, branch transitions, and timezone provenance. They document tested current behavior; they do not validate every school or unsupported correction.

## Limitations

This material describes product behavior, data, and public scope only. It is not a certain forecast, diagnosis, or substitute for professional advice.
