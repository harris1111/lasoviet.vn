# Privacy policy

## A private reading desk, not a public mirror

Your birth date, time, and place aren't data we collect to use elsewhere — they're the one ingredient needed to build your own chart, and we keep only what that requires. This page states plainly what we store, why, and what you control.

## What we store, and why

- **Birth information** (date, time, place): required to calculate the chart; nothing else needs it.
- **Account and payment data:** to deliver the right chart/report to the right order, and to prevent payment fraud.
- **Usage behavior** (visits, sections viewed, reading pace): used to personalize what we show and which offers make sense. Once you tick the consent checkbox in the chart wizard, or sign in, this data is linked to your profile — there is no separate banner or extra consent step beyond that checkbox.
- **IP address:** used for two separate purposes — personalization (kept while your account exists, removed if you request data deletion) and fraud/abuse prevention (kept separately for 12 months).
- **Data not yet linked to an account or consent:** deleted automatically after 30 days if no sign-in or consent follows.

## What we don't do

- We never send your name, exact birth date/time/place, free-text questions, or chart ID to any third-party tool — that data stays on infrastructure we run ourselves.
- We don't run third-party ad pixels today.
- AI interpretation runs on infrastructure Lá Số Việt operates directly, not a third-party service outside our control — your chart doesn't leave a system we manage ourselves to be processed.

## Your rights

You can view your consent status, export your data, or request account deletion anytime from the Privacy section of your signed-in account — no need to contact support to do this.

## Limitations

This material describes product behavior, data, and public scope only. It is not a certain forecast, diagnosis, or substitute for professional advice.
