# Knowledge sources

## Current scope

The source registry identifies brand documents, data contracts, mapping code, fixtures, and evidence rules. Every entry is repository-backed so maintainers can inspect the version that supports published scope.

## How to inspect it

Fixture sources also identify areas without full comparison, including true-solar-time, school differences, and a reference tool's late-Zi limitation. The registry is not independent confirmation of traditional interpretation.

## Limitations

This material describes product behavior, data, and public scope only. It is not a certain forecast, diagnosis, or substitute for professional advice.
