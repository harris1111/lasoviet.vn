# About La So Viet

## Current scope

La So Viet is a platform for building and presenting charts for Vietnamese users. This position requires technical limits, a separation of calculation and interpretation, and no unsupported expert identity.

## How to inspect it

Only one P0 product is public today. Scope, evidence, and privacy are more important than claiming advance knowledge of personal outcomes.
