# Build a Tu Vi chart

## Current scope

This calculator produces a normalized chart with twelve palaces, stable star identifiers, transformations, life and body palace identifiers, and calculation provenance. Those are software facts that can be inspected after a calculation.

## How to inspect it

Time precision remains attached to the input. Fixtures distinguish 00:30 from 23:30 through different adapter indices, while branch-only input remains branch_only. The current method records that it has no true-solar-time correction.
