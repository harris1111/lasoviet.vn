# Frequently asked questions

## Answers

### Does a chart determine the future?

No. The current product presents normalized data, method limits, and reflective material; it does not determine a personal outcome or replace a decision.

### Why does time precision matter?

Exact-minute, branch-only, range, and unknown time have different handling. Unknown time is ineligible, while branch-only input retains an explicit limitation.

## Support boundary

These answers describe published behavior and limits only. They do not create predictions, replace professional advice, or require unnecessary birth information.
