# Tu Vi sample report

## Sample status

This is a stable anonymized sample, not a customer report. Its purpose is to show the report structure without attaching a real person, a public endorsement, or a personal outcome.

## Input scope

The sample accepts a normalized birth profile with declared time precision, timezone provenance, selected ruleSetId ziwei.default, and a chart containing twelve palaces. Unknown time is ineligible; branch-only precision remains visible as a limitation.

## Observations and evidence

Observation one: the chart exposes soulPalaceId and a corresponding palace field. Evidence key: ziwei.identity.life-palace. Observation two: bodyPalaceId resolves to another recorded palace. Evidence key: ziwei.identity.body-palace. Observation three: transformations are present with provenance.ruleSetId. Evidence key: ziwei.identity.transformations.

## Conditional interpretation

Confidence is moderate. These evidence items can support reflection or exploration of how the report is organized; they cannot establish a fixed identity, a diagnosis, a financial result, or a relationship outcome.

## Observable actions

Check the displayed precision and warnings. Compare each evidence key with its fact references. Record a question you want to revisit after reviewing the method. These are observable actions, not promises.

## Limitations

The sample uses no customer data. It preserves documented limits such as input precision, missing true-solar-time correction, and boundaries between calculation, evidence, and interpretation.
