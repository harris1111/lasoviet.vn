# The 14 principal stars in Tu Vi

## What this page can establish

This article explains principal stars from the versioned implementation boundary. the mapping layer converts recognized raw iztro star names into canonical ziwei.star identifiers and records a brightness enum. This is an interoperability mapping, not a published claim about what a star means. It does not add traditional interpretation that is absent from the repository sources.

## Independent example

The solar-term fixture records a principal star identifier in a named palace. The example supports checking mapping output, not inferring a trait or an event.

## Figure specification

Figure 5: a mapping table with raw star field, canonical star identifier, and one of the supported brightness values. The figure is an explanatory diagram, not a generated natal chart and not an instruction to make a personal decision.

## Limits

The current application uses iztro 2.6.0 with ziwei.default, preserves input precision and warnings, and records documented gaps. It does not independently validate true-solar-time correction, every school variation, or traditional meanings outside the mapped fields.
